# Probe: Synchronized Spellcraft (ZNR)
## Cast or play from hand
- target slot (cast): 1..1 of [you_bear, you_giant, opp_flier, opp_tapped, opp_bear, opp_giant]
- target slot (cast): 1..1 of [you, opp]
[cast targets=['you_bear', 'you']]
  event: you_bear is targeted (by card/TARGET)
  event: you is targeted (by card/TARGET)
  event: card moves hand -> stack (by card/CAST)
  event: you casts card
  event: you_bear is dealt 4 damage (by card/DEAL_DAMAGE)
  event: card moves stack -> graveyard (by card/CAST)
  event: you_bear moves battlefield -> graveyard
  event: you_bear dies
  change: card: zone hand -> graveyard
  change: you_bear: zone battlefield -> graveyard; P/T 2/2 -> -
  change: you: hand 6 -> 5; mana R-1 W-4
  change: after the turn ends: opp: mana B-9 C-9 G-9 R-9 U-9 W-9
  change: after the turn ends: you: mana B-9 C-9 G-9 R-8 U-9 W-5
